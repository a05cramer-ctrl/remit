    FAQs 10

    Questions, answered clearly.

    Fees, recipients, routes and payouts, explained before you launch.

    01.Does 80% mean 80% of a trade?

    No. The split applies to creator fees actually collected. Trade volume, price and supply are separate. Fees depend on trading activity; launching a coin does not guarantee revenue.

    02.Can I change the recipient after launch?

    The route is fixed the first time an alias is used. A different destination needs a new reference and a new alias for a new launch. It does not reroute fees from existing coins.

    03.Does a recipient need a crypto wallet?

    No. The payout goes to their approved provider account. Only the creator connects a wallet. A creator's signature does not prove ownership of someone else's payment account, so recipients are reviewed before live payouts.

    04.Why might an allocated balance stay unpaid?

    The next milestone may not be reached yet, the recipient may be awaiting review, or the provider may lack funding. A payment in progress also reserves its allocation until the outcome is confirmed.

    05.Is every payment method available now?

    This site is a demonstration of the product, not a live payout service yet. Listed routes: PayPal, Venmo, Wise, ACH, wire, SEPA, local bank transfer and debit card. Availability depends on country and provider rules.

    06.What happens if a payment result is unknown?

    The request is kept and the funds stay reserved until it is reconciled. An uncertain response is never treated as proof that no money moved.
